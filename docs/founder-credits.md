# Founder credits and visual sources

## Attribution model

The capabilities deck presents AETHER, FAIRYLAND, and Emily Was Here as Storyverse case studies. Those three are displayed in “Storyverse work.”

MAIA, ChalkNotes, and Wherefore Art Thou, Juliet? are placed under Phil Olarte in “Founders’ work,” with the roles already documented in the project data. This section covers earlier and independent work, without asserting that every project predates the company. Their individual detail pages carry a founder credit and use a Person contributor in structured data; Storyverse is not described as their creator.

Alba’s selected founder credits link to her existing portfolio instead of creating new Storyverse project pages. Her role is named on each card. Sleep No More credits her character contribution, not authorship of the entire production. The Raven is described as a collaborative work, not a solo-created production. Alba's historical credit name Ava Lee Scott is explained where relevant.

The founding story's introduction through Vance and connection at Cherry Lane Theatre come from the conversation supplied by the owner on November 15, 2026. No company founding date, full name for Vance, or specific founding performance is invented.
